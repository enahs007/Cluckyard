Cluckyard Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026 · Package com.cluckyard.game

Cluckyard is a single-player farmyard hen game. You do not create an account. We do not run ads, and we do not sell or share personal information.

What we collect

Cluckyard does not collect names, emails, location, contacts, photos, payment info, or advertising IDs.

Your best grain score is stored only on your device (local app storage). Uninstalling the app deletes that score. It is never sent to our servers because Cluckyard has none for this build.

Internet permission

The Android package may declare an Internet permission as part of the standard app shell. This version of Cluckyard does not require a network connection to play and does not upload gameplay data.

Children

Cluckyard is a mild cartoon game (a hen hiding from foxes and other animals). It does not include social features, chat, or in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

If you download Cluckyard from Google Play, Google may process data under its own policies (for example install stats). That is Google, not Cluckyard.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
