# Cluckyard

A cozy farmyard hen game. Peck grain, burst-flap like a real hen, and get home to the coop before dusk.

## Play

**Five dawns** — five days, each harder. Roost every night to finish the week.

**Endless yard** — days keep coming. Foxes multiply, the hawk dives sooner, dusk comes faster.

Reach the coop at the far end of the yard and go **Home** before night. Early roosts start the next day and keep your grain. Night in the open ends the run.

Foxes hunt the ground. Hawks watch the sky. Flap in short bursts — hens do not hover.

## Controls

**PC**

- A / D or arrows — walk
- W, Space, or up — flap
- S or down — peck
- E — home (at the coop)
- Esc — pause

**Phone**

- Stick — walk
- Flap / Peck / Home
