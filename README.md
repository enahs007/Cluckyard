# Cluckyard

A cozy farmyard hen game. Peck grain, burst-flap like a real hen, and get home to the coop before dusk. Five dawns. Each day the yard gets harder.

## Play

Reach the coop at the far end of the yard and go **Home** before night. Early roosts start the next day and keep your grain. Night in the open ends the run.

Foxes hunt the ground. Hawks watch the sky. Flap in short bursts — hens do not hover.

## Controls

**PC**

- A / D or arrows — walk
- W, Space, or up — flap
- S or down — peck
- E — home (at the coop)
- Esc — pause

**Phone**

- Stick — walk
- Flap / Peck / Home
