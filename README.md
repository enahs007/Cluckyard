# Cluckyard

A cozy farmyard hen game. Peck grain, burst-flap like a real hen, hide from hunters, and get home to the coop before dusk.

## Play

**Five dawns** — five days, each harder. Roost every night to finish the week. Fox and hawk only.

**Endless yard** — days keep coming. A new predator joins every five dawns (dog, bobcat, coyote), dusk comes faster, and the hawk dives sooner.

Reach the coop at the far end of the yard and go **Home** before night. Early roosts start the next day and keep your grain. Night in the open ends the run.

Foxes, dogs, bobcats, and coyotes hunt the ground. Hawks watch the sky. Rabbits and doves share the yard but never hurt you.

**Hide** by standing still in a bush or a tree. Hunters lose sight until you move or flap.

Flap in short bursts — hens do not hover.

## Controls

**PC**

- A / D or arrows — walk
- W, Space, or up — flap
- S or down — peck
- E — home (at the coop)
- Esc — pause
- Stand still in a bush or tree — hide

**Phone**

- Stick — walk
- Flap / Peck / Home
- Hold still in cover — hide
